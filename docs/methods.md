# Growing methods: pots, beds and climbers

How to choose between a pot, a raised bed, in-ground planting and a trellis. The crop-by-crop table covers the 20 starter crops on the [Darwin](start/darwin.md) and [Sydney](start/sydney.md) pages.

> **How reliable is this page?** Pot sizes come from one council in Adelaide ([Salisbury Council, growing in pots and containers](https://www.salisbury.sa.gov.au/assets/files/assets/public/general_documents/live/healthy_living/living_well_in_the_living_city/pack_e1_growing_in_pots_and_containers_final.pdf)) and two Sydney sources. None is a Darwin source, so treat sizes as a starting point in the Top End. Where no source names a crop, the table says so instead of guessing. The photo and video links open search results that I have not checked one by one.

## Pick a method

| Method | When it suits | Source |
|---|---|---|
| In the ground | Cost-effective and simple to set up, if your soil is workable. Add organic matter such as compost first. | [Bunnings, beginner's guide to starting a vegetable garden](https://www.bunnings.com.au/diy-advice/garden/planting-and-growing/beginners-guide-to-starting-a-vegetable-garden) (a retailer); [nt.gov.au, tips for new gardens](https://nt.gov.au/environment/home-gardens/tips-for-new-garden) |
| Raised bed | Poor or heavy soil. The Darwin and Katherine sheet advises beds 10 to 15 cm high so water drains. | [Bunnings](https://www.bunnings.com.au/diy-advice/garden/planting-and-growing/beginners-guide-to-starting-a-vegetable-garden); [NT DAF IS4](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0003/228090/is4_vegetable_calendar_darwin_katherine_08.pdf) |
| Pots | Renters, balconies, courtyards. Fewer large pots beat many small ones. | [City of Sydney, tips to start an edible garden](https://news.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/articles/tips-to-start-an-edible-garden) |
| Hanging basket | Tomatoes, peas and beans can grow in them. | [NSW Environment, small spaces](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/about-us/get-involved/sydney-nature/gardens/small-spaces) |
| Trellis or mesh | Climbers such as beans, peas and cucumber. | [NSW Environment, small spaces](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/about-us/get-involved/sydney-nature/gardens/small-spaces) |

- **Sun:** the Bunnings page says to aim for at least six hours a day, and in the tropics to filter the midday sun with shade cloth or overhanging trees ([Bunnings](https://www.bunnings.com.au/diy-advice/garden/planting-and-growing/beginners-guide-to-starting-a-vegetable-garden)). The City of Sydney says most edibles need about 4 to 6 hours ([City of Sydney](https://news.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/articles/tips-to-start-an-edible-garden)). Both are shown.
- **Pots in detail:** depth, potting mix, drainage and watering are on the [small spaces page](start/small-spaces.md).
- **Sow or transplant:** each crop's method is in the sowing tables on the [Northern Territory](states/nt.md) and [NSW and ACT](states/nsw-act.md) pages, and on its crop page.

## Gaps

- No Darwin source gives pot sizes. Salisbury is in Adelaide, so sizes are untested in the tropics.
- Sweet potato, lemon grass, kang kong and radish have no pot guidance in the sources read.
- I could not play or vet any video. Video advice may suit another climate or conflict with the sourced pages. When it does, follow the sourced page and the label on any product.
- Photo links are searches, not chosen images. Licences vary by file, so check the licence on the file page before reusing a photo.
