# Companion crops

Which plants are said to grow well next to which, with a link on every name: a crop in this guide opens its crop page, and any other plant opens its note below. Use it with the crop pages: each of the 20 crop pages has a Companion crops section.

> **How reliable is this page?** Weak overall. The pairings come from the NT Government's [companion planting sheet VF11 (2005)](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf), Hornsby Council's [guild and companion planting fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf), SGA's [Asian greens page](https://www.sgaonline.org.au/asian-greens/) and Diggers' [lettuce guide](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce). None of them cites trials. The [principles page](principles.md#companion-planting-evidence-versus-folklore) rates the evidence: flowering strips are supported, marigold rotation is supported for nematodes, and most other pairings are folklore or untested. The sources also disagree in places, and those are marked below. Where no source names companions for a crop, the crop page says so.

## Plant notes

### Beans

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says beans fix nitrogen for neighbours and suit most vegetables and herbs, with corn as support. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) lists onions, garlic, corn and potatoes. The NT sheet lists onions as a bad neighbour, so the two sources disagree.

**Evidence:** Nitrogen: real, but mostly helps the next crop, not neighbours. Beans on corn is mainly physical support (see the principles page). The other pairings are untested.

**In this guide:** [Darwin crop page](crops/darwin-snake-beans.md), [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-bush-beans.md).

### Peas

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists carrots, radish, cucumber, corn, beans and squash as companions. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) adds potatoes and says avoid onions and garlic.

**Evidence:** Not tested. Peas, like beans, fix nitrogen mainly for the following crop.

**In this guide:** [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-peas.md).

### Corn

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says corn suits beans, peas, cucumber, squash and pumpkin, giving support and shade, and that it may confuse some pests. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) also lists it with tomato, lettuce, cucumber and beans. The NT sheet says avoid corn with tomatoes, so the sources disagree on tomato.

**Evidence:** Three Sisters (corn, beans, squash) is plausible but thin. No trial was found for the other pairings.

### Sunflower

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says sunflowers shade bush beans and cucumber, and attract pollinators. It lists sunflowers as a bad neighbour for potatoes.

**Evidence:** Shade is plausible. Not tested.

### Squash and pumpkin

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says they suit corn (support and shade), marigold and nasturtium, and are bad with potatoes.

**Evidence:** Three Sisters is plausible but thin.

### Celery

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says celery helps leeks, cauliflower, cabbage and tomatoes, repels white cabbage butterflies, and suits bush beans. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) lists it with lettuce.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

### Carrot

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists carrots with peas and lettuce. [SGA](https://www.sgaonline.org.au/asian-greens/) lists carrots with Asian greens and [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) with lettuce.

**Evidence:** Not tested. A carrot-and-tomato claim traced to a 1975 book title, so it is not used here.

### Beetroot

**What the sources say:** [SGA](https://www.sgaonline.org.au/asian-greens/) lists beetroot with Asian greens, and [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) and the [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) list it with lettuce.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

### Onions and garlic

**What the sources say:** The sources disagree. [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists onions as a bad neighbour for corn, cabbage, peas and beans, and as a good one for tomatoes. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) says onions and garlic suit beans but not peas. [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) lists chive-family onions with lettuce.

**Evidence:** Weak or mixed. Marigold and mint did not repel onion pests in trials, and no source supports an allium and legume clash (principles page). Treat as folklore.

### Chives

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists chives with tomato, and around fruit tree bases to put off climbing insects. [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) lists chive-family onions with lettuce.

**Evidence:** Chinese chives reduced bacterial wilt of tomato in one 1999 study, known only from a blog summary. Otherwise untested.

### Parsley

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says parsley helps tomato and attracts bees and ladybirds. [SGA](https://www.sgaonline.org.au/asian-greens/) and [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) say avoid parsley with Asian greens and lettuce.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

### Dill

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says dill helps the cabbage family and attracts wasps that eat cabbage caterpillars.

**Evidence:** Overseas work found dill with eggplant reduced Colorado potato beetle by attracting predators. For cabbage it is untested in the sources read.

### Aromatic herbs

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says aromatic herbs deter cabbage worms for the cabbage family, but lists cucumbers with aromatic herbs as a bad pair. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) also says avoid them with cucumber.

**Evidence:** Not tested. Pest-repelling herbs are weak in the trials read.

### Cabbage family

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says cabbage-family crops like aromatic herbs, dill, celery and tomatoes. [SGA](https://www.sgaonline.org.au/asian-greens/) lists cabbage as an Asian greens companion. [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) says avoid broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower and kale with lettuce.

**Evidence:** Not tested for these pairings.

### Marjoram

**What the sources say:** [SGA](https://www.sgaonline.org.au/asian-greens/) lists marjoram with Asian greens.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

### Marigold

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says marigolds keep soil free of nematodes and suit tomato, eggplant and squash. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) says marigolds suit everything.

**Evidence:** Supported only as a full-bed rotation crop dug in before planting (California trials on root-knot nematode). Scattered plants are unlikely to work, and the insect-repelling claims are weak (principles page).

### Nasturtium

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says nasturtium deters aphids, bugs, beetles and whitefly, and suits radish, squash and tomato.

**Evidence:** Weak or mixed. One review calls it a pretty aphid nursery unless the pests are removed (principles page).

### Borage

**What the sources say:** [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) lists borage with strawberries.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

**In this guide:** [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-strawberries.md).

### Petunias

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says petunias with tomatoes repel tomato worm and asparagus beetle.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

### Asparagus

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists asparagus with tomato and parsley. No other source was read.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

### Eggplant

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists beans and marigolds as companions. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) says avoid eggplant with tomato.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

### Potatoes

**What the sources say:** [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) lists potatoes with beans and peas, and says avoid them with cucumber and tomato. [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists potatoes as a bad neighbour for pumpkin, squash, tomato, cucumber and sunflower.

**Evidence:** Tomato and potato share early and late blight, which is a rotation reason to keep them apart (principles page).

### Tomato

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says parsley, basil, chives, onions, cucumber, marigold and nasturtium suit tomato. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) says corn suits it and avoid potato, eggplant, calendula and rosemary.

**Evidence:** See the individual plants. The two sources disagree on corn.

**In this guide:** [Darwin crop page](crops/darwin-cherry-tomato.md), [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-cherry-tomato.md).

### Cucumber

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists beans, peas, corn, sunflowers and radish. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) lists corn and says avoid aromatic herbs and potatoes.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

**In this guide:** [Darwin crop page](crops/darwin-cucumber.md), [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-cucumber.md).

### Radish

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists peas, lettuce, cucumber and nasturtium.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

**In this guide:** [Darwin crop page](crops/darwin-radish.md), [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-radish.md).

### Lettuce

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) lists carrot, cucumber and radish. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) adds corn, beans, celery, beetroot and radish. [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) adds beans, chive-family onion and strawberry, and says avoid broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower, kale and parsley.

**Evidence:** Not tested.

**In this guide:** [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-lettuce.md).

### Basil

**What the sources say:** [NT sheet VF11](https://daf.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/227606/vf11.pdf) says basil improves tomatoes and repels flies and mosquitoes.

**Evidence:** Basil improving tomato flavour or yield is folklore: blind taste tests found no difference. Basil did reduce thrips on tomato in some studies (principles page).

**In this guide:** [Darwin crop page](crops/darwin-basil.md), [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-basil.md).

### Strawberries

**What the sources say:** [SGA](https://www.sgaonline.org.au/asian-greens/) and [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) list strawberries as a companion for Asian greens and lettuce. [Hornsby fact sheet](https://hornsby.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/52512/HSC00272-EGFS-Guild-and-Companion-Planting_FA.pdf) lists borage.

**Evidence:** Not tested. A separate rule: do not plant strawberries where tomato-family crops grew in the last three years (SGA).

**In this guide:** [Sydney crop page](crops/sydney-strawberries.md).

### Flowers for beneficial insects

**What the sources say:** [Diggers](https://www.diggers.com.au/blogs/learn/how-to-grow-lettuce) lists achillea, coreopsis, French marigold and zinnia near lettuce to attract ladybirds and lacewings.

**Evidence:** Related to the best-supported practice below, but these four were not tested.

### Alyssum, cornflower and buckwheat strips

**What the sources say:** Australian field trials put 1 m flowering strips beside brassicas. Beneficial insects increased and were still raised at least 15 m into the crop, and diamondback moth parasitism more than doubled. Alyssum flowers longest, cornflower is best for parasitoid wasps, and buckwheat is fast but short-flowering and frost tender ([Hort Innovation VG16062](https://www.horticulture.com.au/globalassets/hort-innovation/resource-assets/vg16062-boosting-beneficials-in-your-vegetable-crop.pdf)).

**Evidence:** Supported (moderate). This is the best-evidenced companion practice in the guide. The trials were on brassicas, so for other crops it is a reasonable bet, not a proof.

## Gaps

- No source read gives companions for chilli, sweet potato, kang kong, lemon grass, rocket or silverbeet.
- No Top End or tropical trials of companion planting were found.
- Hornsby's sheet names no publisher or date on the page read. It gives no studies.
- The NT sheet's pairings are general, for example for beans rather than snake beans.
