# Biosecurity and weeds

> **How reliable is this page?** It collects the rules and declared weeds already sourced on the state pages. The sources are government pages, but several were read through summaries, some are dated (the NT quarantine list is 2019 text), and no state's full declared-weed list was read except as noted. Rules change during outbreaks. Treat this page as a map of what to check, and confirm with your state agency before you move plants, soil or fruit.

## The short version

- **Fruit and plants can be restricted when moved**, even between your own properties. Fruit fly, banana disease, fire ants and a few vegetable pests drive most of the rules.
- **Check before you move or post anything:** fruit and vegetables, nursery stock, banana suckers, soil, mulch, compost, potting mix and used machinery.
- **A plant can be a gardener's friend in one state and a declared weed in another.** Absence from a list you read is not clearance. Always check the current statewide list and your local council.
- **Cover crops and hedge plants count.** Several popular green manures and hedge plants are declared in some places (see the table below).

## Other biosecurity notices from the sources reviewed

- **Myrtle rust** infects Myrtaceae, including lilly pilly, eucalypts and tea tree. Check plants before buying, don't move infected plants, and don't use green waste bins for them. Lemon myrtle is not named on the NSW DPI page, so treating it as susceptible is an inference ([NSW DPI myrtle rust](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/biosecurity/plant/insect-pests-and-plant-diseases/myrtle-rust)). It is now in the NT ([nt.gov.au](https://nt.gov.au/industry/agriculture/food-crops-plants-and-quarantine/nursery-and-garden-industry)).
- **Varroa mite (beekeepers, NSW):** NSW has moved to management. All beekeepers must register and test every 16 weeks ([NSW DPI Varroa FAQ](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/emergencies/biosecurity/current-situation/varroa-mite-emergency-response/varroa-frequently-asked-questions)).
- **Exotic pests flagged by the NT:** polyphagous shot-hole borer and tomato brown rugose fruit virus ([nt.gov.au](https://nt.gov.au/industry/agriculture/food-crops-plants-and-quarantine/plants-and-quarantine)).

## Cover crops and hedge plants

- In the NT, physic nut, castor oil plant, sicklepod, candle bush and sensitive plants are declared, so leave them out of any hedge or green-manure list. [inference] See [Northern Territory](states/nt.md).
- Johnson grass (NT Class C) is a sorghum relative: use named forage sorghum hybrids and stop volunteer seed escaping. [inference]
- Don't dump green waste: feral olive spreads by birds, foxes and contaminated soil or green waste (Green Adelaide, above).
- Whether pigeon pea, sunn hemp, lablab, castor oil or leucaena are restricted in Queensland was not confirmed. Check local council pest plant lists. See [Queensland](states/qld.md) and [principles](principles.md).
