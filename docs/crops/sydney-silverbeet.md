## What you need

- Seed
- A pot or bed with 20 to 30 cm of soil

## Growing methods

- **In a pot:** Silverbeet suits a medium pot, 20 to 30 cm deep[186](../references.md#r186).
- More on pots, soil and water: [small spaces, pots and renting](../start/small-spaces.md).

## How to grow it

1. Sow seed 15 mm deep, 25 to 50 cm apart.
2. Seedlings come up in 5 to 10 days.
3. Pick outer leaves as it grows.

## Care guide

- **Starting:** Sow seed or transplant seedlings, then thin to the spacing and eat the thinnings. In cool and warm temperate climates plant from spring to autumn[287](../references.md#r287).
- **Temperature:** No figures are given[287](../references.md#r287).
- **Sun and shade:** Full sun to part shade[287](../references.md#r287).
- **Watering:** Every 2 to 3 days, or more often in hot weather. It tolerates dry spells but does better with regular water. Wilting plants usually revive with a thorough watering[287](../references.md#r287).
- **Feeding:** Liquid feed regularly in the growing season. No product, rate or interval is given. Enrich the soil with compost before planting[287](../references.md#r287).
- **Care:** Mulch with sugarcane or pea straw. Start picking outer leaves after 8 to 10 weeks, twisting each stalk near the base[287](../references.md#r287).
- **Common problems and fixes:** Cercospora leaf spot: grey spots with brown edges, worse in hot humid weather. Snails and slugs: barriers, traps or pellets[287](../references.md#r287).
- **Not found:** Spacing, feeding rate and pot size.
